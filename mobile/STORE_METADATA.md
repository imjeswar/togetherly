### Full Store Description
**Your relationship deserves little moments of magic.**

Togetherly is an intimate, private relationship sanctuary crafted exclusively for two consenting adults who want to foster a deeper, more intentional connection. Inspired by Apple-level simplicity, editorial warmth, and premium glassmorphic dark design, Togetherly is your private space away from social media noise.

#### ✨ Key Experiences:
- **Intimate Daily Questions**: Explore 100+ deep, romantic, and playful prompts. Answers remain hidden until both of you reply, unlocking a synchronized reveal.
- **Genuine End-to-End Encrypted Love Notes**: Send letters sealed with AES-256-GCM encryption. Decrypted exclusively on your partner's device.
- **Shared Live Drawing Canvas**: Sketch sweet doodles, heart notes, and drawings on a synchronized canvas.
- **Daily Photo Moments**: BeReal-style dual perspective snaps to share what you're seeing in the moment.
- **7 Synchronized Couples Games**: From trivia and "This or That" to gratitude and truth-or-dare style intimate prompts.
- **Mutual Date Matcher**: Swipe together on romantic date night ideas. When you both say yes, it's a match!
- **Milestone Memory Lane**: Preserves photos, drawings, notes, and milestones with dynamic anniversary and streak counters.

#### 🛡️ Privacy & Security Without Compromise:
- **Zero Advertising & No Data Selling**: Your relationship data is sacred.
- **Client-Side E2EE**: Hardware-isolated private keys via `SecureStore`.
- **Biometric Sanctuary Lock**: Unlock with Face ID, Touch ID, or fingerprint.
- **Two-Factor Authentication**: Optional TOTP protection.
- **Offline-First Architecture**: View memories and sketch offline; changes auto-sync when connection returns.
- **Home-Screen Widgets**: Native Android and iOS WidgetKit widgets to keep your partner close on your home screen.
